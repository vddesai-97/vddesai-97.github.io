6 months of living in this van and here’s what we know:
- no driving after dark
- Google maps always try to get you to take the back roads but that’s almost never better 
- Staying in one place saves money even if you eat out a little more than if you’re constantly moving 
- Starting and ending the day with self-independent time is crucial to sharing our life in a small space 
- When we find ourselves lost and unsure, that just means we need to reset our thinking
