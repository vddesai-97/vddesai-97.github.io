When we first arrived in Costa Rica and learned that people here are sensitive to large dogs, and dogs in car, and dogs in national parks, we were worried that we may not end up being able to do much here. With some research and thinking outside the box, I think we were able to turn around that worry and make the most of Costa Rica such that we actually hit most of our goals! 

What did we do? Leave notes in the windshield stating that arya loves to bark but she has cool air andf water. Tie her to the outside of the car when it was sunny so people see she is fine and that she has water. Tell the parking attendants that she is in the car and she is fine.
