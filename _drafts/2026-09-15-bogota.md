Having lived on the road for nearly a year now, the word "home" has a multitude of meanings for us. In the most immediate sense, our van is, of course, our home on wheels, where we go to sleep and to reset. Given that we are constantly on the move, we will often refer to the street or neighborhood where we parked as "home" as well, so that "heading home" could indicate simply returning to the next-door park or a regularly frequented cafe. Of course, home also means the U.S., although we may be referring to our family homes (Indiana and Minnesota) or adopted state of North Carolina. And finally, occasionally, we will be lucky enough to be invited into someone else's home so that it becomes our temporary one for a short period, a wonderful gift to break up some of the difficulties **solitude** of life on the road.

In that sense...

Our time at home **(in the U.S.) was not static. Constantly on the move in some form or fashion. We visited 8 states and 9 homes, and traveled via both cars and airplanes. There was also a shift between the first half of our trip and the second half. A shift from wanting to be back on the road to being nervous about going back to the Stinkbug. This shift led to some reflections within ourselves and together of how we wanted the next couple of years to look like for us. While we plan to finish our time in South America slowly and luxuriously, we are also looking forward to coming back, settling down and having a community within reach.



Cajica the town of extreme kindness. While we tried our best to pay for the space we take it, it was also nice to be cared for and taken care of such taht there is no worry. Of that. We truly appreciated and are extremely grateful for our time with the Sastres. A week before we flew out to the US, we had stopped in a town to meet up with some friends for lunch the next day. Turns out, parking anywhere in this town was complicated as unbeknownst to us, the police are likely to tow you car for overnight parking, and the streets were very small. We tried our luck at the plaza of Cajica where we saw a bunch of farmer market stands but after enquiring with the security guard, we found out that would not be possible.
