Our time at home was not static. Constantly on the move in some form or fashion. We visited 8 states and 9 homes, and traveled via car and airplanes. There was also a shift between the first half of our trip and the second half. A shift from wanting to be back on the road to being nervous about going back to the Stinkbug. This shift led to some reflections within ourselves and together of how we wanted the next couple of years to look like for us. While we plan to finish our time in South America slowly and luxuriously, we are also looking forward to coming back, settling down and having a community within reach.
