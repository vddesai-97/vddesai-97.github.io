The struggles of living the van life as the people we are. 
Being two people where one is satisfied by routine and schedules and plans and the other used to be that way but has slowly become someone who loves the flexibility and freedom and planning things on the go. I am still someone who plans things way in advance and I started loving the freedom of no schedule while still having a full-time job. But these two type of people can clash and we do clash in our current living situation. Where we have to share mode of transportation and errands and our lives pretty much together. More so than just living together and sharing a home where you go to your own work and have that independence. There are days where the one person may unintentionally drive the day much to the dissatisfaction of the other. There are days where we are intentional in doing our own thing and maintaining independence while also showing up together as team.
