The struggles and beauty of living the van life as the people we are. 

**we used to be type-A people, schedules, and plans and all that. Both of us are learning to be more flexible. but that growing process isn't linear, and doesn't happen the same across two different people. this can lead to clashes and conflicts**

Being two people where one is satisfied by routine and schedules and plans and the other used to be that way but has slowly become someone who loves the flexibility and freedom and planning things on the go. I am still someone who plans things way in advance and I started loving the freedom of no schedule while still having a full-time job. C would like it noted that he is also learning to be more flexible but there are growing pains.

But these two type of people can clash and we do clash in our current living situation. Where we have to share one mode of transportation and errands and our lives pretty much together. More so than just living together and sharing a home where you go to your own work and have that independence. There are days where one person may unintentionally drive the day much to the dissatisfaction of the other. This occurs sometimes in difference of productivity or desire to move on to the next location or even in when to do errands.

There are days where we are intentional in doing our own thing and maintaining independence while also showing up together as team. Days where we pick up the slack put down by the other, so as to keep our lives moving forward, even if the Stinkbug is stagnant that day.
