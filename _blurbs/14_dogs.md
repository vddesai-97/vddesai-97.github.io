When we first arrived in Costa Rica, we learned that people here are sensitive to dogs being left in cars, and with Arya not allowed to enter national parks, we were worried that we may end up not being able to do much here. With some research and thinking outside the box, I think we were able to turn around that worry and make the most of Costa Rica so that we actually hit most of our activities! 

What did we do? Sometimes we would leave notes in the windshield stating that Arya loves to bark but she has cool air and water. Other times we would tie her to the outside of the car when it was sunny so people would see she is fine with water, air, and the ability to crawl under the shade of the car. When possible, we would inform the parking attendants that she was in the car so they were aware she might bark, but is alright. All good tips we learned from an expat during our first week in Costa Rica :)
